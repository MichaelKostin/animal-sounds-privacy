Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

App Name: Animal Sounds: Learn & Play

Developer: Mikhail Kostin

1. Overview

We take your and your child’s privacy seriously. This app is designed for children and their families. We do not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information. Our goal is to provide a safe, educational, and fun experience without compromising your privacy.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Names or email addresses
  • Photos, videos, or audio recordings
  • Location data
  • Behavioral or usage analytics
  • Advertising or tracking information

3. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

The app offers optional in-app purchases and subscriptions to unlock additional content. These purchases are handled securely through the official Apple App Store and Google Play billing systems.

We use a service called RevenueCat to securely manage and validate your in-app purchases and subscriptions. RevenueCat only receives non-personal data related to purchase history (such as product identifiers, transaction dates, and subscription status). This information is used solely to maintain access to premium content and synchronize purchases across your devices.

RevenueCat does not collect personally identifiable information such as name, email, or location. All data is encrypted in transit and processed in accordance with Apple and Google privacy requirements.

For more information, you can review RevenueCat’s privacy policy:
🔗 https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

4. Third-Party Services

Other than the app stores, RevenueCat for subscription functions, and Firebase Crashlytics for crash diagnostics, Animal Sounds: Learn & Play does not include third-party behavioral analytics, attribution, advertising, or cross-app tracking SDKs. We do not sell personal information or share it with advertisers or data brokers.

We use Firebase Crashlytics, provided by Google, solely to identify crashes, diagnose technical problems, maintain security and stability, and improve the app’s operation. Crashlytics automatically processes crash reports, exception names and messages, stack traces, relevant application state, crash time, app identifier and version, device model and operating-system information, and random app-installation and session identifiers. We do not set a Crashlytics user ID or custom attributes, and we do not intentionally attach names, email addresses, advertising identifiers, precise location, contacts, photos, recordings, purchase information, gameplay progress, or custom account identifiers to crash reports. Firebase Analytics is not included in the app, so Crashlytics does not receive Analytics breadcrumb logs. Crashlytics information is not used for advertising, behavioral profiling, or tracking across apps or services.

5. Parental Gate

Any links to subscription screens, external websites, or social media are protected by a parental gate. This ensures that children cannot accidentally access content or perform actions intended for adults.

6. Data Security and Retention

All purchase-related data processed by RevenueCat is encrypted in transit and stored securely. We do not retain or store this data ourselves. You can request deletion of RevenueCat-associated data by contacting us via email (see below).

Crashlytics information is encrypted in transit and at rest. Firebase generally keeps crash stack traces and associated installation identifiers for 90 days before beginning the process of removing them from live and backup systems.

7. Your Consent

By using the app, you consent to this privacy policy and to the limited use of purchase-related data as described above.
